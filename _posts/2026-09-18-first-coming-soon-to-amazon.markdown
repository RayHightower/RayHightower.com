### Capitalism and Service

Capitalism is all about service. The most successful capitalists attract customers by offering a better product or service than their competitors. These business leaders perform so well that customers consistently return for more.


Capitalism creates opportunities where everyone can win. When members of the economy compete on the basis of service, everyone can win.

### Five Pillars

FIRST examines five pillars of capitalism and entrepreneurship: Focus, Initiative, Relationships, Speed, and Trust. FIRST explores these pillars through the hard-won lessons of the author and other seasoned entrepreneurs. New business leaders will gain wisdom by studying the five pillars. Seasoned business pros, those with scars to prove it, will smile to see their years of experience reflected in this book.
