FIRST: Celebrate Capitalism & Win

Available on Kindle, Audible, or paperback.

FIRST is a celebration of capitalism and a salute to the entrepreneurs who improve the world by serving others.

Capitalism is all about service. The most successful capitalists attract customers by offering a better product or service than their competitors. These business leaders perform so well that customers consistently return for more.

Capitalism creates opportunities where everyone can win. FIRST will show you how.
