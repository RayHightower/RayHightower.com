Every achievement in Ray Hightower's life has roots in parental lessons. Mom and Dad taught:

* By example.
* With advice like "go and do something constructive".
* Through poetry. 

Here are two poems that the sons were required to memorize and recite from a young age.

* **[If](/if-rudyard-kipling/)** by Rudyard Kipling

* **[It Couldn’t Be Done](/done/)** by Edgar Albert Guest
